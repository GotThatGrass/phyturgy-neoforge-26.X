## Overview

**Phyturgy** is a Minecraft mod focused on botanical magic, inspired by the ancient Chinese *I Ching*.

Uncover the hidden magic within plants, extract their power, and combine ancient symbols to brew potions and cast spells. Discover strange flowers, towering trees, mysterious fungi, and more as you explore diverse biomes and venture into the depths of the world.

*Every plant has a secret.*

## Development

Phyturgy is currently in early development. Features and mechanics are subject to change.

## Addons

Addons and compatibility projects are welcome! Expand Phyturgy with new plants, trigrams, hexagrams, potions, spells, and more.
